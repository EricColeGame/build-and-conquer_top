## How Do Build and Conquer Levels Work?

Levels matter because they determine which buildings you can access as your country grows. A community building list reports unlocks from Level 1 through Level 4, but the available sources do **not** explain how to earn levels or confirm a complete level cap. Treat the unlocks below as a planning reference, and check your in-game build menu before committing resources.

The [official Build and Conquer Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/103767961452227/Build-and-Conquer) describes a beta strategy game built around managing a city, researching technology, and defending it. It warns that updates are ongoing, so building availability may change.

## Reported Building Unlocks by Level

The following examples come from the community-maintained [Roblox Build and Conquer Wiki civilian buildings list](https://roblox-build-and-conquer.fandom.com/wiki/Civilian_Buildings). They are **community-sourced**, not an official progression chart, and the table is not a complete list of every building at each level.

| Level | Reported civilian building unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Poor House, House, Solar Panel, Wind Turbine, Water Tower, Shop, and Middle Class Apartment |
| 2 | Low Income Apartment, Coal Power Plant, Strip Mall, and Basic Office Building 1 |
| 3 | City Apartment, Large Supermarket, and Low Rise Office Building |
| 4 | Mid Rise City Apartments, High Rise City Apartment, and several larger office buildings |

A higher level does not necessarily replace earlier options. The community list, for example, places both basic housing and the Middle Class Apartment at Level 1.

## What to Prioritize as You Build

Start by checking whether your housing has the power and water it needs. The community building list shows those requirements for residential buildings, while the official description identifies workers, population, power, and water networks as parts of city management.

Next, consider your workforce before adding commercial buildings. The [Build and Conquer city management wiki](https://the-slop-company.fandom.com/wiki/City_Management) describes housing as a source of workers and commercial buildings as a source of money that needs those workers. As more buildings unlock, compare their stated needs with what your city can supply rather than choosing solely by level.

## What the Sources Do Not Confirm

Neither the official description nor the provided community pages specifies the experience requirement, action, or milestone for reaching each level. The civilian building list also leaves some categories without detailed entries. That means it cannot establish a full unlock list, a fastest leveling method, or a maximum level.

For the most reliable answer about your current save, use the in-game level indicator and build menu. Community lists can help you anticipate options, but the beta game itself is the better check for what is available now.

## FAQ

### What are the Build and Conquer levels?

The community civilian building list labels unlocks at Levels 1, 2, 3, and 4. The available evidence does not confirm whether Level 4 is the maximum.

### How do you level up in Build and Conquer?

The provided sources do not specify a leveling method or experience requirements. Check the game’s current interface for progression details rather than relying on an unverified leveling route.

### Does every building unlock at a new level?

No. The community list reports multiple buildings available at Level 1, with additional examples at later levels. It does not provide a complete, officially confirmed unlock schedule.
