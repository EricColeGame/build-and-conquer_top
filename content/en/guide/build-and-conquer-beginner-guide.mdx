## Start With a City That Can Function

An empty plot can tempt you to build an army immediately, but your city supports everything that comes later. In Roblox Build and Conquer, the practical beginner priority is to establish power and water, add housing for workers, and then grow your income through commercial buildings. The [official Build and Conquer experience page](https://www.roblox.com/games/103767961452227/Build-and-Conquer) describes the game as a beta, so check building requirements in-game before committing to an expansion.

## Follow a Simple Starting Order

1. **Plan space for your first neighborhood and services.** The game includes roads, housing, power, water networks, farms, and shops. Leave room to add them as your city grows, without assuming that every building has the same placement requirements.
2. **Provide power and water.** The [Roblox Build and Conquer Wiki's civilian building list](https://roblox-build-and-conquer.fandom.com/wiki/Civilian_Buildings) lists solar panels and wind turbines as early power options and says housing needs both energy and water. Check the service coverage and available capacity shown in your current game.
3. **Add housing before relying on shops.** Housing supplies population and workers. Commercial buildings need workers as well as utilities, so placing a shop without the people or services to support it may leave your income plan incomplete.
4. **Build commercial income, then reassess.** Shops are an early commercial option in the wiki's building list. After adding one, check your money, workers, power, water, and happiness before repeating the pattern.

This is a dependency-based starting order, not a guaranteed fastest build. The sources do not establish an optimal layout or a fixed number of each building to place.

## Grow the Economy Without Creating New Shortages

Treat every new building as both a benefit and a demand on your city. More housing can provide workers, but it also needs services. More commercial buildings can generate money, but they draw on workers, power, and water. Check those balances before spending on another expansion.

Once the basics are stable, farms, resource production, factories, and research become relevant to a larger country. The [The Slop Company Wiki's city management overview](https://the-slop-company.fandom.com/wiki/City_Management) describes agriculture as a food source and resources and industry as part of production. Its separate main page contains older alpha-stage language, however, so use the current in-game requirements to decide what to build next rather than treating wiki listings as a complete beta build order.

## Prepare for Conflict After Your City Can Support It

Build and Conquer also involves technology, manufactured vehicles and missiles, troop deployment, alliances, and defense. Those goals depend on the economy and production you develop first. Before shifting spending toward military buildings, make sure your city can continue meeting its civilian needs and funding further construction.

There is no verified beginner troop composition or universal attack timing in the provided sources. A useful rule is to expand your military in response to the resources and threats you can actually see, while protecting the city that pays for it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I build first in Build and Conquer?

Start by establishing power and water for housing, then add residents to supply workers for commercial buildings. Check the current in-game requirements and coverage as you place each building.

### How do beginners make money?

The official description identifies money as a resource to manage, and the city management wiki describes commercial buildings as a major income source. Build the housing and services those businesses need before expanding them.

### Should I build an army right away?

Prioritize a functioning city first. The official game description connects a strong economy with military success, while also making defense an important part of protecting what you build.

### Are wiki building costs and requirements always current?

Not necessarily. Build and Conquer is described as a beta, and wiki pages may reflect different stages of development. Use them to understand building roles, then confirm costs, unlocks, and requirements in-game.
