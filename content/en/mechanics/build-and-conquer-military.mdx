## How does the military work in Build and Conquer?

A military in Build and Conquer starts with a city that can support it. The [official Build and Conquer Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/103767961452227/Build-and-Conquer) says players can research technology, manufacture vehicles and missiles, deploy troops through tunnels to the mainland, form alliances, and fight over strategic areas. It also makes clear why the civilian side matters: your economy supports a war effort, while your military protects the city you built.

The official description does not specify troop costs, research requirements, combat controls, or the exact order in which military options unlock. Treat any precise build order as unverified unless you can confirm it in the game.

## Build an economy before expanding your forces

The official page identifies money, workers, resources, power, research, and production as systems you manage while growing your country. It also names farms, shops, factories, power plants, and water networks among the structures used to develop a city. Before committing heavily to military production, check whether your city can keep those systems running.

The community-maintained [City Management page on The Slop Company Wiki](https://the-slop-company.fandom.com/wiki/City_Management) describes housing as a source of workers, commercial buildings as a source of money, and resource and industrial buildings as part of production. That is useful context, but the game’s beta status means wiki building lists and requirements may not match the current version.

## Research, manufacture, and deploy

The official description confirms three military activities: researching technology, manufacturing vehicles and missiles, and deploying troops through tunnels to the mainland. It does **not** establish that every vehicle or missile uses the same research path, or that troops, vehicles, and missiles share a deployment method.

A practical way to approach these systems is to check what your current research and production menus require, then prepare those resources before starting a project. When you are ready to move troops, look for the tunnel route described by the official page rather than assuming they can deploy directly from your city.

## Defend your city and consider alliances

Build and Conquer includes city defense, alliances, and battles with other nations for strategic areas, according to its official description. Those are connected decisions: committing resources to a fight may leave less available for the city you need to protect.

The community wiki’s [City Management military section](https://the-slop-company.fandom.com/wiki/City_Management) lists barracks, military factories, missile launchers, and defensive batteries. It does not provide enough detail to verify their current costs, effectiveness, or unlock conditions. Check the live game before planning a defense around a particular building.

## Build and Conquer military FAQ

### Do I need a strong economy to build a military?

The official description says the economy is important to winning wars and the military protects what you build. It confirms that players manage money, workers, resources, research, and production, but does not give a minimum income or city size for starting a military.

### How do I send troops to the mainland?

The official page says troops deploy through tunnels to the mainland. It does not explain the controls or requirements for doing so.

### Can I manufacture missiles and vehicles?

Yes. The official description confirms both. Specific models, production costs, and research paths are not established by the available official information.
