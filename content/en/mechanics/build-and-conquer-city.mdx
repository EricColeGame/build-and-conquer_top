## How Does City Building Work in Build and Conquer?

Your city is the foundation of your economy and military in Build and Conquer. Starting from an empty plot, you develop housing, roads, utilities, farms, shops, and factories while managing workers, resources, happiness, and money. The [official Build and Conquer Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/103767961452227/Build-and-Conquer) describes the game as a beta, so treat specific building values as subject to change.

The central challenge is keeping those systems connected. Housing supplies people and workers; utilities support buildings; commercial activity brings in money; and production and research help prepare your country for conflict.

## Supply Housing With Water and Power

Residential buildings need water and energy to function, according to the community-maintained [Roblox Build and Conquer Wiki’s civilian buildings list](https://roblox-build-and-conquer.fandom.com/wiki/Civilian_Buildings). That makes utility coverage a practical consideration whenever you add a neighborhood.

The wiki lists solar panels, wind turbines, and power plants as energy sources. It also lists a water tower with a limited service area and a desalination plant that provides city-wide water. These are community-listed details, not a guarantee that every value or service rule remains unchanged in the current beta. Check the in-game building information before committing to a large expansion.

## Match Workers to Income Buildings

Housing does more than increase population: it provides the workers that commercial buildings require. The community wiki lists shops, gas stations, malls, banks, and offices with worker and utility needs, alongside their income. Its listings also show that some apartments require steel or carry upkeep costs.

A useful way to plan a Build and Conquer city is to consider each proposed business alongside the housing, water, and power needed to run it. Expanding commercial space alone may not solve an income problem if you cannot staff or supply the new buildings.

## Build an Economy That Supports Defense

The official game description connects city growth to research, manufacturing, troop deployment, alliances, and defense. It also identifies farms, factories, power plants, and water networks as parts of a growing country. In other words, military expansion depends on more than placing military buildings: you must continue managing the city and resources behind them.

As your city grows, review what it can support before shifting spending toward vehicles, missiles, or other military goals. The official description does not provide a universal build order, and the best next investment depends on your city’s current needs.

## FAQ

### What should I focus on first in a Build and Conquer city?

Focus on the connections between housing, water, power, workers, and income. The sources establish that these systems depend on one another, but they do not verify a single best opening build order.

### Do houses need water and power?

Yes. The community wiki lists water supply and energy as requirements for its residential buildings. Verify the current requirements in-game, since Build and Conquer is in beta.

### Where does city income come from?

The official description includes money management as part of city growth. The community wiki identifies commercial buildings as a major income source and lists worker requirements for them.
