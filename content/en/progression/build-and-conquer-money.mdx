## Where Does Money Come From in Build and Conquer?

Running short of money can stall your city before it is ready to expand or defend itself. In Build and Conquer, the practical route to more income is to develop your city, especially its commercial buildings, while supplying the workers and services they need. The [official Build and Conquer experience page](https://www.roblox.com/games/103767961452227/Build-and-Conquer) confirms that money, population, workers, power, and resources all matter as your country grows.

The [Build and Conquer City Management wiki](https://the-slop-company.fandom.com/wiki/City_Management) identifies commercial buildings as the main source of money and says residential buildings provide the workers needed to place them. That makes housing and utilities part of an income plan, even when they are not the main reason you are building.

## Build an Income Base Before Expanding

A sensible early priority is to connect each new money-making building to the support it needs:

1. **Provide housing for workers.** Commercial buildings need workers supplied by residential buildings, according to the City Management wiki.
2. **Supply power and water.** The community-maintained [Civilian Buildings reference](https://roblox-build-and-conquer.fandom.com/wiki/Civilian_Buildings) lists both among the needs of shops and several other commercial buildings.
3. **Add commercial buildings your city can support.** Check their worker, energy, water, and construction requirements before committing money.
4. **Leave room in your budget for upkeep.** Compare a building’s listed income with its recurring costs rather than treating the headline income figure as profit.

These are planning priorities, not a guaranteed build order. The official page describes Build and Conquer as a beta with ongoing updates, so check current in-game requirements before relying on a wiki listing.

## Compare Income With Costs

The Civilian Buildings wiki gives a useful example: it lists the **Shop** at a $400 construction cost, with $65 income and $3 upkeep per pay cycle. It also lists requirements of two workers, 10 energy, and water supply. On those figures, the shop’s listed income exceeds its own upkeep by $62 per cycle, but that does **not** account for the cost of supplying its workers, power, or water.

The same reference lists higher-income options, including the Strip Mall and office buildings, with larger worker and utility requirements. A bigger income number alone does not make a building the right next purchase. Check whether your city can operate it and whether its construction cost would leave enough money for essential services.

## Keep Growth Sustainable

When income feels slow, look for a missing dependency before adding another shop. Check whether you have enough workers, whether residential and commercial buildings have the water and power they need, and whether new upkeep costs are consuming the extra income you expected.

That matters beyond city expansion. The official game description ties a strong economy to military strength, while also describing research, manufacturing, and defense as parts of the game. Building a dependable income base gives you more flexibility to pursue those goals without neglecting the city that supports them.

## Build and Conquer Money FAQ

### What is the main source of Build and Conquer money?

The City Management wiki identifies commercial buildings as the main money source. The official experience page also confirms that managing money is part of growing your country.

### Do houses make money?

The Civilian Buildings wiki lists pay-cycle income for some residential buildings. Housing’s other important role is supplying the population and workers needed to support commercial growth.

### Why shouldn’t I build the highest-income building immediately?

Higher listed income can come with greater construction costs, upkeep, worker demand, or utility needs. Compare the full requirements with what your city can currently supply.

### Are the wiki’s prices and income figures guaranteed to be current?

No. They are community-maintained figures, and the official game page says Build and Conquer is in beta and receives updates. Use the wiki for planning, then confirm costs and requirements in the game.
