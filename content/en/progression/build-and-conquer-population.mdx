## How does population work in Build and Conquer?

Building more housing is the way to increase **Build and Conquer population**, but a home needs water and power to function. Population matters because residential buildings also provide the workers your city needs for commercial buildings. The [official Build and Conquer experience page](https://www.roblox.com/games/103767961452227/Build-and-Conquer) identifies population, workers, happiness, and utilities as parts of city management; it does not publish a population-growth formula.

## How to grow your population

Start by placing residential buildings, then make sure they have water and enough power. The [Build and Conquer city-management wiki](https://the-slop-company.fandom.com/wiki/City_Management) says housing supplies citizens and workers and that residences need water to function.

As your city expands, check those services before adding another block of homes. Plan for jobs, too: the same wiki says commercial buildings need workers from residences. More housing can increase your available population, but the sources do not establish that every new citizen becomes an available worker or that population rises automatically over time.

## Which housing adds the most population?

The [Roblox Build and Conquer Wiki’s civilian-building list](https://roblox-build-and-conquer.fandom.com/wiki/Civilian_Buildings) reports these population amounts. Treat them as **wiki-listed figures**, not guaranteed values for every beta update.

| Housing | Listed population added | Listed unlock level |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Poor House | 3 | 1 |
| House | 5 | 1 |
| Two Story House | 8 | 1 |
| Low Income Apartment | 14 | 2 |
| City Apartment | 20 | 3 |
| High Rise City Apartment | 48 | 4 |

A larger population figure is not the only consideration. The wiki lists energy and water requirements for every home in this table, while some apartments also have upkeep or material requirements. Check the building details in your current game before committing resources.

## What to check if population is not increasing

If you have placed housing but are not seeing the population you expect, check the requirements the sources actually document:

- **Water:** Confirm that the residence is supplied. The wiki says residential buildings need water to function.
- **Power:** Compare your available energy with the home’s requirement. The civilian-building list gives an energy need for each housing type.
- **Building status:** Check whether construction has finished; the wiki lists build times for housing.

These are checks, not a confirmed diagnosis for every stalled city. Build and Conquer’s official page describes the game as a beta with ongoing updates, so use the current in-game building information if it differs from a wiki entry.

## FAQ

### How do I increase Build and Conquer population?

Build residential housing and supply it with the water and power it needs to function. Different housing types have different wiki-listed population amounts.

### Does population provide workers?

The city-management wiki says residential buildings provide both citizens and workers. It does not specify a universal worker-to-population ratio.

### Is the biggest apartment always the best choice?

Not necessarily. Compare its population gain with its unlock level, utility needs, and any listed upkeep or materials. The highest-population option in the table also has higher requirements than the early homes.
